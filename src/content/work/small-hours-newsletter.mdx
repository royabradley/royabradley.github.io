Small Hours began as a note to twelve friends about what I was reading. It is now a weekly letter with a paid tier that covers its own costs.

## Format

One essay, three links, and a closing line. The shape has not changed since the first issue, and readers say that is part of the appeal.

## Growth

There was no launch. Each letter ended with a line asking readers to forward it to one person, and most months, someone did.
