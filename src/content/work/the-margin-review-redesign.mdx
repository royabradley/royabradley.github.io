The Margin Review had grown the way many small journals do: a new widget every issue, a sidebar that nobody remembered adding, and body text squeezed to make room for both.

## The brief

Make the essays easier to read without losing the journal's voice. No new features, and nothing the three-person staff would have to maintain.

## What changed

- The measure came down to about 65 characters, and the leading went up.
- Pull quotes replaced the sidebar. They carry the same weight with none of the clutter.
- Footnotes moved back to the foot of the essay, where readers expect them.

> The complaint we heard most was that the site felt quieter. Nobody meant it as a complaint.

## Results

Time on page roughly doubled over the first two issues, and the editors stopped receiving emails asking where the article had gone.
