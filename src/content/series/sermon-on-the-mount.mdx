## The shape of the sermon

Matthew frames the sermon with a mountain, a seated teacher and gathered disciples (5:1–2), and closes it with the crowds astonished that he taught "as one having authority" (7:28–29). Inside that frame the teaching is carefully built. "The Law and the Prophets" appears at 5:17 and again at 7:12, bracketing the body of the sermon, and the Lord's Prayer sits near its center (6:9–13).[^1]

## Key terms

- *Makarios* (5:3–11): [your gloss — "blessed" in the sense of the Hebrew *ʾašrê*, the flourishing of those set right with God, rather than a mood.]
- *Dikaiosynē* (5:6, 10, 20; 6:1, 33): [righteousness as covenant faithfulness lived out, not only a status.]
- *Teleios* (5:48): [whole, undivided, mature — echoing *tāmîm* in Genesis 17:1 and Deuteronomy 18:13.]
