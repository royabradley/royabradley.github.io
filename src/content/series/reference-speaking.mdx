This series documents the speaking record, published as the Pulpit and the Lectern, by being part of it. The hub you are reading is a series entry, and the pages linked below are speaking items, each written as a working example of its kind and form. Copy from them.

<Callout type="note" title="Dev only">
These pages live in `src/content/speaking/reference/` and `src/content/series/reference-*.mdx`. They show in `pnpm dev` and are left out of every production build by the loader patterns in `src/custom/speaking/collections.ts`, so they never need `draft: true`.
</Callout>

## The model

The record has two collections. Everything on its pages is derived from their fields.
It is published in two sections, named for where the words were spoken, and `kind` decides which:

| Section | Holds | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pulpit | Sermons, and sermon series | `/pulpit/` |
| Lectern | Lessons, talks, devotionals and presentations, and lesson series | `/lectern/` |
| Record | Everything from both, by year; linked from About, not in the nav | `/record/` |


| Collection | One entry is | Folder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `speaking` | One sermon, lesson, talk, devotional or presentation | `src/content/speaking/` |
| `series` | One series hub: an overview, followed by a generated table of its items | `src/content/series/` |

The schema is in `src/custom/speaking/collections.ts`; queries, labels and URLs are in `src/custom/speaking/utils.ts`; the components are in `src/custom/components/speaking/`. The route files in `src/pages/pulpit/` and `src/pages/lectern/` (and `src/pages/record.astro`) only pass entries to those components; the paths themselves are built in `src/custom/speaking/routes.ts`.

## Fields of a speaking item

| Field | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | Yes | Page title and list entry |
| `description` | Yes | One sentence, 160 characters at most; shown under the title and in lists |
| `date` | Yes | When it was preached, taught or given; orders every list |
| `kind` | Yes | `sermon`, `lesson`, `talk`, `devotional` or `presentation`; the label and the per-kind listing |
| `form` | No (`manuscript`) | `manuscript`, `notes` or `slides`; the shape of the page |
| `passage` | No | What it expounds: "Matthew 5:3–12", or "Heidelberg Catechism, Q. 1". Leave it out for a topical lesson or talk |
| `series` | No | The id of a series entry; makes the item part of that series |
| `part` | No | Its place in the series; without it, items fall back to date order |
| `place` | No | Congregation or venue |
| `occasion` | No | "Christmas Eve", "Sunday School", "Men's retreat" |
| `audio`, `video`, `slides` | No | Links out; audio also gets the browser's own player |
| `draft` | No (`false`) | `true` keeps it out of production builds |

A series entry has `title`, `description`, `kind` (`sermon` or `lesson`), and optionally `passage`, `place` and `draft`.

## Kinds and forms

`kind` says what the item was; `form` says what the page holds. Any kind can take any form, and the page follows the form:

<Figure caption="The form, not the kind, decides how a page is laid out.">

| Form | Body | Recording or deck | Contents box |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manuscript | Full prose, with sidenotes and figures | After the series contents, as a companion | Yes, from three headings up |
| Notes | An outline or condensed points | First, under the title | No |
| Slides | A short summary | The deck link first, under the title | No |

</Figure>

## Files and URLs

File names become URLs. A leading number orders files in the folder and is dropped from the URL, so `01-the-blessed.mdx` publishes as `the-blessed`.

In the URLs below, `<section>` is `pulpit` or `lectern`.

| Page | URL | Comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A section | `/<section>/` | Its series, then its items, newest first |
| A kind's listing | `/lectern/lessons/` | Lectern items of that kind; built only once one exists. Sermons need none: the Pulpit is their listing |
| A series hub | `/<section>/<series>/` | `src/content/series/<series>.mdx` |
| An item in a series | `/<section>/<series>/<item>/` | Any file in `speaking/` whose `series` names it |
| A standalone item | `/<section>/<item>/` | Any file in `speaking/` without a `series` |
| The record | `/record/` | Every published item, by year |

Series membership comes from the `series` field, not the folder. Keeping a series' files in a folder of the same name is a convenience, nothing more.

## Safeguards

The build stops, with a message that names the problem, when:

- an item's `series` names a series that doesn't exist;
- an item has a `part` but no `series`;
- an item sits in a series of the other section: a sermon in a lesson series, or a lesson in a sermon series;
- a description runs past 160 characters;
- a series, a kind's listing and a standalone item in the same section would share one URL.

An item in a draft series is unpublished too, so a series can be prepared in full and released at once by removing one `draft: true`.

## From the vault to the site

A note from SPEAKING RECORD becomes a page in four steps:

1. Copy it into `src/content/speaking/` (or a series folder) and change `.md` to `.mdx`.
2. Make sure its properties match the fields above; Obsidian properties and Astro frontmatter are both YAML.
3. Replace `[[wikilinks]]` with ordinary links or plain text, and Obsidian callouts (`> [!note]`) with `<Callout>`.
4. Keep footnotes as they are; `[^1]` works in both, and becomes a sidenote here.
