A talk is anything given outside worship and Sunday School: a retreat address, a conference session, a lecture. It often has no `passage`, and the metadata line simply leaves it out.

## The slides form

With `form: slides`, the page leads with the links to the deck and any recording, then a short summary. Keep the summary to what someone deciding whether to open the slides would want: the argument in two or three sentences, and who it was for.

Link the deck as a PDF where you can. A PDF opens on every device and keeps your typography; a link to Keynote or Google Slides depends on the reader having access.
