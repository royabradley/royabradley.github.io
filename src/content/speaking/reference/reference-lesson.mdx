A lesson works like a sermon in its fields, but its subject varies more. Set `passage` to whatever the lesson is about:

| Lesson on | `passage` | Example title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Scripture text | `Ephesians 6:1–4` | Children, obey your parents |
| A confession or catechism | `Heidelberg Catechism, Q. 1` | Our only comfort |
| A topic of Christian life | Leave it out | Keeping the Lord's Day |

For a topical lesson, the title carries the subject, and the metadata line simply has no passage. `occasion` is usually "Sunday School", which the line shows at the end.

## Standalone or in a series

This lesson has no `series`, so it lives at `/lectern/reference-lesson/`, has no part number, and its footer links back to all lessons. To put lessons in a series, create a series entry with `kind: lesson`; it appears at the Lectern, its hub reads "Sunday School series", and its table lists lessons. A sermon can't join it: the build stops if a series mixes the two sections.

## An example lesson outline

1. The household as a place of discipleship (6:1–4)
2. Obedience "in the Lord": what the phrase includes and limits (6:1)
3. The promise attached to the fifth commandment (6:2–3)
4. Fathers: provoke not, but bring up (6:4)
5. Discussion: what does catechesis at home look like this week?
