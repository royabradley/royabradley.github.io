A devotional is short: a text read and opened before a meeting, a class or a meal. Its page should be short too. Notes are usually the right form; a manuscript only if it was written out.

## An example

1. I lift up my eyes to the hills (121:1)
2. He who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep (121:4)
3. Your going out and your coming in (121:8)
