A presentation differs from a talk by its purpose: it informs or proposes something to a particular group, usually with slides, often with a handout. Use `kind: talk` for an address and `kind: presentation` for a briefing.

## Handouts and other files

A handout is another link. Put it in the body, under the summary:

- [Handout: a catechism reading plan for households (PDF)](https://example.com/reference-handout.pdf)
