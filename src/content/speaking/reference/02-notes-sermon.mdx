Notes are for sermons whose fullest form is the recording. The page leads with the audio, then the series box, then the outline. There is no contents box, because an outline is its own contents.

## How to write notes

1. Keep the outline the congregation heard: main points as a numbered list, sub-points nested under them.
2. Put the verse reference at the end of each point, in parentheses.
3. Add a sentence or two under a point only where the recording alone would leave a reader lost.

## An example outline

1. Salt that keeps its savor (5:13)
   1. Salt preserves and seasons
   2. Salt that has lost its taste is good for nothing
2. A city set on a hill (5:14)
3. A lamp on its stand (5:15)
4. Good works that glorify the Father (5:16)
