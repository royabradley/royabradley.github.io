I write slowly, mostly about reading, handwriting, and the small mechanics of how text sits on a page. This site is where those essays end up once they have stopped changing every morning.

Nothing here is sponsored, and there are no ads. If an essay mentions a book or a pen, it is because I used it and had something to say about it.

## About this site

The site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and the Pilcrow theme. The body face is Newsreader, the interface face is Inter, and the only color besides ink and paper is the red a rubricator would have used for the ¶ mark.
