This site is made slowly, by hand, from a small study in East Texas. It is meant for reading at the pace of a book, not a feed.

## Type

The text is set in Newsreader, a serif made for long reading on screens, from Production Type. Captions and the interface are set in Inter, by Rasmus Andersson, and code in JetBrains Mono. All three are open-source typefaces, served from this site rather than from a font service.

## Making

Essays begin as notes in Obsidian and are prepared for the site in Visual Studio Code. The design is Pilcrow, an Astro theme by Ondelva, adapted here. The site is built with Astro and hosted on GitHub Pages.

## What the site does not do

There is no advertising, no comments, no share buttons, and no cookies. Visits are counted anonymously, without cookies and without identifying anyone, and the numbers are not shown. The [privacy page](/privacy/) has the details.

## The epigraph

The lines on the home page are from Elizabeth Barrett Browning's _Aurora Leigh_ (1856), Book VII.

## The season

The line at the foot of every page names the season of the church year, reckoned by the calendar of the Western church. It changes at midnight, Texas time, as the site is rebuilt each day.
