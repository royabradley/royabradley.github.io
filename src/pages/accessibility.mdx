## The aim

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What that means here

- Text and interface colors are chosen to meet AA contrast in both light and dark mode.
- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus ring and a link to skip to the content.
- Images have text alternatives, and form fields have labels.
- The layout holds together down to 360 pixels wide and at 200% zoom.
- On wide screens, footnotes also appear as notes in the margin; on narrow screens and to screen readers they remain ordinary numbered footnotes.

## Known issues

- Hebrew and Greek words are displayed with fonts already on your device, so on some systems they may look different from the surrounding text.

## Reporting a problem

Please describe the page and what went wrong on the [contact page](/contact/). I'll fix what I can as soon as I can.
