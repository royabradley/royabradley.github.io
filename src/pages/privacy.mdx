_Last updated 11 November 2026._

## In short

This site does not track you. It counts visits anonymously and without cookies, and there is no advertising and there are no comments.

## What the site keeps

Nothing about you. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is stored in your own browser and never sent anywhere. The fonts and the site search are served from this site, so reading it makes no requests to Google. The one outside service a page calls is the visit counter.

## Visit counts

I use [Umami](https://umami.is) to count visits, so I can see which pages are read and how readers found them. For each page you open, it records the page's address, the site that linked you here, your browser, operating system and type of device, and your country. Your country is worked out from your IP address, which Umami does not store. It sets no cookies and does not follow you to other sites, and I see only totals, never individual readers. If your browser blocks it, the site works the same.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical information about each visit, including IP addresses, in its server logs, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). I don't see those logs.

## The contact form

When you send a message, your name, email address and message pass through [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com), which forwards them to my email, under its own [privacy policy](https://web3forms.com/privacy). I use your address only to reply to you, and I never add it to a list.
