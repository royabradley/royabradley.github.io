## Essays

The essays in the Study are © Roy A. Bradley. You are welcome to quote short passages, with credit and a link to the essay. For anything more, such as republishing a whole essay, translating it or using it in print, please [ask first](/contact/).

## Sermons and teaching

Sermons, lessons and talks are offered more freely. You may read, print, share, preach, teach and adapt them in the ministry of the church without asking. Credit is welcome but not required. Please don't sell them or publish them for profit.

Like most preaching, they draw on the work of many others (commentators, theologians, preachers and hymn writers), not always named at the point of use. The freedom above covers my own words only. Quoted material remains its authors', under their terms.

## Not pastoral counsel

The essays and sermons here are written for a general audience. They are not personal pastoral counsel, and they are no substitute for the care of a pastor and elders who know you. If you are facing a question of faith, conscience or life, please speak with your own pastor and church. Nothing here is legal, medical or financial advice.

## No warranty

Everything here is provided as is. Links to other sites are offered for your convenience and are not endorsements.

## Changes

These terms may change. The date of the latest revision is noted at the top of this page.
