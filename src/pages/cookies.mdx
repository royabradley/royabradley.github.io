_Last updated 11 November 2026._

## Cookies set by this site

None. The only thing stored in your browser is your light or dark mode preference, kept in local storage and never sent anywhere.

## Third parties

None. Visits are counted with [Umami](https://umami.is), which sets no cookies, and the site has no advertising, comments or embedded media, so no other service sets cookies here. If that ever changes, this page will change first.

## Changing your mind

You can clear local storage from your browser settings at any time. The site will fall back to your system's color scheme.

See also the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
